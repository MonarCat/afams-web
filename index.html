The Origin

Chai the Way It Was Meant to Be

SuChai was born from a simple frustration — great tea shouldn't require great effort. After years of perfecting the formula, we developed a proprietary processing method that delivers bold, satisfying chai without the sieve, without the mess, without compromise.

Two varieties give you freedom — Ready to Drink for the quick cup, Ordinary for the slow traditional brew over the jiko.
